# Derivatives

Deck 02 of the [Paul Wilmott Introduces Quantitative Finance &mdash; Companion Series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/Wilmott_QF_Hub).

**Live presentation:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/Wilmott_QF_02_Derivatives/

A guided tour of chapter 2 of *Paul Wilmott Introduces Quantitative Finance*
(2nd edition, Wiley, 2007) &mdash; the contracts that turn an underlying $S$
into a derivative: calls, puts, payoff diagrams, and the strategy zoo.

## What's inside

- What an option is &mdash; the right but not the obligation; the four cardinal positions
- Strike, expiry, premium, moneyness, intrinsic vs time value, European / American / Bermudan exercise
- Payoff diagrams for long/short calls and puts and the buyer&ndash;writer mirror
- Writing options and margin; standard market conventions
- Put&ndash;call parity from no-arbitrage: $C - P = S - K e^{-rT}$, and the synthetic-stock interpretation
- Binaries (cash-or-nothing, asset-or-nothing) as building blocks
- Vertical spreads &mdash; bull and bear
- Straddles, strangles and risk reversals &mdash; trading volatility, not direction
- Butterflies, condors, calendar spreads, and a brief on warrants, convertibles, LEAPS and OTC options
- **Interactive option strategy composer** &mdash; add long/short call/put legs with sliders for strike and premium, watch the total payoff and P&amp;L update, and read off net premium and breakeven prices
